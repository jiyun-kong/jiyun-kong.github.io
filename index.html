Hello, I am

Jiyun Kong.

Researcher in Computer Vision & Multimedia

My research focuses on how visual systems can better understand and reconstruct dynamic scenes. I am particularly interested in event-based vision, neural video compression, and the use of event cameras as complementary sources of temporal information.

My recent work explores event-guided learned video coding and event-based scene synthesis, including approaches to motion modeling and inter-frame residual alignment. I have also participated in research collaboration at EPFL in Switzerland.
